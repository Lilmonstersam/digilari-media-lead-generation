About Duracube

Duracube, a leading provider of wet area joinery solutions, engaged in a comprehensive digital marketing strategy to enhance its online presence, drive traffic, and increase conversions. Its buyers include architects, designers, builders and project managers. This case study outlines the challenges faced, solutions implemented, and results achieved across organic search, the website and LinkedIn Ads.

Challenges

Keyword Targeting and SEO Optimisation: Duracube needed to optimise its content for a diverse set of keywords related to bathroom and toilet partitions, compact laminate, and accessibility standards, ensuring visibility across various search terms.

Technical SEO and Site Structure: Ensuring the website was technically sound, with optimised meta tags, schema markup, and a logical site structure to improve search engine rankings and user experience.

Content and Local SEO: Creating relevant content that addresses the needs of local audiences in multiple regions, including Melbourne, Sydney, Brisbane, and Adelaide.

Analytics and Performance Monitoring: Implementing effective tracking and analysis of web traffic and user behaviour to continually refine the marketing strategy.

Reaching Specifiers on LinkedIn: Duracube wanted to promote its Specifier Portal to senior people in architecture, design and building management. Its first LinkedIn campaigns used static and carousel ads optimised for website conversions, running at a 0.53% click-through rate and $1.95 per click.

Solutions

Search and website

Digilari embarked on a comprehensive optimisation strategy to tackle these challenges:

Keyword Optimisation & Content Strategy

Implemented a targeted keyword strategy focusing on high-traffic and niche keywords such as “disabled toilet standards australia,” “toilet partitions brisbane,” and “compact laminate.” Created and optimised content around these keywords, ensuring alignment with user search intent and addressing frequently asked questions and common issues.

Technical SEO Improvements

Conducted technical audits and implemented fixes for site speed, mobile usability, and crawl errors. Added schema markup for local businesses and sitelinks searchbox to enhance search engine visibility and click-through rates. Implemented universal search bars and sticky headers to improve navigation and user experience.

Local SEO & Content Marketing

Developed a content plan focusing on localised keywords and services, such as “toilet partitions sydney” and “toilet partitions melbourne.” Published case studies, how-to guides, and industry news to engage the local audience and establish Duracube as an authority in the industry.

Performance Tracking & Analytics

Set up comprehensive tracking through Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to monitor traffic acquisition, user behaviour, and engagement metrics. Regularly reviewed and optimised campaigns based on performance data, focusing on high-performing channels and addressing underperforming areas.

LinkedIn Ads

On LinkedIn, Digilari changed the format, the bidding and the targeting:

Video-first creative

Replaced static and carousel ads with single video Sponsored Content: specifier-focused videos promoting the 15-year warranty and architect-ready tools such as Revit BIM models, then launch videos for the new First Nations Range. The best videos reached a 6.2% to 7.5% click-through rate.

Click-focused bidding

Switched from conversion bidding, which had too little data to learn from, to landing page click optimisation so LinkedIn could find the people most likely to visit.

Senior decision-maker targeting

Targeted 17 job titles at Senior, Manager, Director, Partner and Owner level, layered with relevant skills, industries and fields of study such as architecture. Companies with 1 to 10 staff and competitor employees were excluded.

Sector pages and tighter seniority

In October 2026, targeting moved to job function and Manager-and-above seniority at companies with 51 or more staff, sending people to industrial, commercial and sports facility pages.

Results: Search and website

Outstanding Performance Metrics and Achievements

  • Increased Organic Traffic: Organic search traffic increased by 50.38%, with users growing from 15,509 to 23,908 during the comparison period. Significant growth in key regions, with traffic from Melbourne, Sydney, and Brisbane showing marked improvements.
  • Enhanced User Engagement: Improved average session duration and pages per session, indicating better user engagement and content relevance.
  • Successful Keyword Rankings: Achieved top rankings for targeted keywords such as “disabled toilet standards australia,” “toilet partitions brisbane,” and “compact laminate.” Effective keyword strategy led to increased visibility and higher click-through rates for key search terms.
  • Improved Technical Performance: Technical SEO enhancements resulted in better mobile usability, and fewer crawl errors, contributing to overall site health and user experience. Schema markup and enhanced site structure facilitated better indexing and richer search results.
