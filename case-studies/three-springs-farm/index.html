Three Springs Farm

Harvesting Growth with Targeted Paid Ads Across Google and Meta

About Three Springs Farm

Nestled in Australia’s Northern Rivers, Three Springs Farm is a certified organic producer with a focus on medicinal herbs and sustainable farming. Their flagship product, Complete•3 Turmeric Liquid Extract, reflects their commitment to a “food as medicine” philosophy, bringing together traditional knowledge and scientific backing to promote health. Digilari manages its paid advertising on both Google and Meta: Google captures people already searching, while Meta finds new customers likely to buy.

Challenges

Google Ads: Three Springs Farm faced substantial obstacles in digital advertising, primarily due to high conversion costs that restricted ad visibility and effectiveness. The challenge was twofold: enhancing ad delivery to reach a wider yet targeted audience within a tight budget, and addressing gaps in online customer engagement.

Meta Ads: With a budget of about $300 a month, Meta needed to bring in new customers profitably, not just traffic. The previous Meta campaign was already returning 6.92x, so the task was to keep finding new buyers across Australia without letting the cost per purchase climb.

Solutions

Google Ads

Digilari embarked on a comprehensive optimisation strategy to tackle these challenges:

Google Ads Optimisation

Conducted rigorous ad copy testing to identify messaging that would engage the target audience effectively.

Strategic Bid Adjustments

Implemented strategic bid adjustments to maximise reach without overspending, optimising visibility among high-intent users.

Performance Max campaigns

Launched through a newly established Google Merchant Centre account, allowing products to appear in Google Shopping searches. This integration used machine learning to distribute ads across multiple channels, enhancing targeting and efficiency.

Meta Ads

On Meta, Digilari focused spend on people close to buying and kept testing the creative:

Near-buyer lookalike audience

Built the main audience from a lookalike of people who added to cart but didn't buy, layered with health, wellness, organic food, fitness and gardening interests. Recent buyers were excluded so budget went to new customers. This ad set took 85% of spend and delivered 291 orders at 7.53x.

Credibility and offer-led creative

Single image ads and a 10-card carousel led with the product's credentials (certified organic, grown on the family farm) and the subscription and free-delivery offers. The carousel returned 10.2x.

Seasonal Christmas push

A separate, time-limited ad set promoted the returning 500ml bottle for Christmas, optimised to checkouts. It delivered 47 orders at 7.38x.

Facebook and Instagram together

The campaign ran across both placements and let Meta allocate spend. Instagram took 12% of spend and returned 14.4x, against 6.5x on Facebook. A September 2025 creative refresh with subscription-saving messaging reached 16.5x.

Results: Google Ads

Significant Gains in Paid Search Metrics

  • Revenue Growth: Year-over-year revenue generated from paid ads surged by 230%, showcasing the effectiveness of the optimised campaigns in converting clicks into sales.
  • Paid Search Traffic: Paid search sessions increased by 96%, demonstrating heightened visibility and engagement among potential customers.
  • Key Events Growth: Key events triggered by users from paid search channels grew by 112%, indicating improved interaction and engagement with site content.

Results: Meta Ads

Meta-Attributed Sales at a 7.5x Return on Ad Spend

  • Revenue: $33,062 in Meta-attributed online sales from $4,404 in ad spend over 15 months (December 2024 to February 2026).
  • Orders: 338 orders at $13.03 each, with average order value up from $90.04 to $97.82.
  • Return on ad spend: 7.51x, up from 6.92x on the previous campaign. Instagram placements returned 14.4x.
  • Best month: April 2025, with 44 orders and $4,735 in revenue from $299 in spend (15.8x).
