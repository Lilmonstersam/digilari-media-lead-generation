About Streamline Carports

Streamline Carports designs and builds steel carports for homeowners across Auckland, with sustainable PurlinDek steel roofing and a 10-year structural warranty. Meta Ads is one of its main sources of quote requests, sending people to the carports page to request a quote or get in touch.

Challenges

Streamline needed a steady flow of quote requests from Meta at a cost that made sense for a considered home purchase.

At launch (May to October 2024), a broad Auckland campaign was costing NZ$148 per enquiry and bringing in about 2 enquiries a month. The audience was too wide, and the creative wasn't giving homeowners a clear reason to choose Streamline over a cheaper alternative.

Solutions

Digilari rebuilt the audience and creative, then kept moving budget towards what the results supported:

Refined lookalike audience

Built an ad set from a 1% lookalike of recent website visitors, layered with homeowner, home improvement and vehicle-owner interests. Run side by side with the original broad audience, it cost 42% less per lead (NZ$61.44 against NZ$105.49).

Durability-led creative

Rebuilt the ads around what matters to carport buyers: strength, rust protection, PurlinDek steel roofing and a 10-year structural warranty. Meta's flexible format tested three images with several headlines and primary texts in each ad.

Budget that follows results

Paused the weaker broad ad set and moved its budget to the refined one. When GA4 showed a separate traffic campaign's visitors weren't engaging, 70% of its budget moved into lead generation.

Creative fatigue monitoring

Tracked cost per lead month by month and refreshed copy and creative when results slipped, rather than letting ageing ads absorb more budget.

Results

Lower Cost per Lead, Ten Times the Enquiries

  • Cost per lead: down 62%, from NZ$148.22 at launch to NZ$56.57 over the last 12 months.
  • Lead volume: from about 2 enquiries a month at launch to about 20 a month, with 234 Meta-reported leads in the last 12 months.
  • Click efficiency: click-through rate up from 1.14% to 1.56%, and cost per click down from NZ$1.09 to NZ$0.82.
  • Website activity: GA4 recorded 80 quote and contact form submissions and 54 phone-link clicks from Meta visitors over the same 12 months.
