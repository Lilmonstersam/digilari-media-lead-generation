About CV Sheds

CV Sheds designs and builds custom steel sheds across Central Victoria. Google Ads captures people already searching for a shed. Meta's job is different: reach property owners earlier and bring them to the website before the busy summer build season.

Challenges

The client's brief was a single line: plan a January campaign along the lines of “New Year, New Shed”, with the concept left to Digilari.

The campaign needed to bring low-cost, relevant visitors to the site from the right towns, without spending on Melbourne or audiences unlikely to build a shed. The New Year's campaign finished at a 1.85% click-through rate and $0.44 per landing page view.

Solutions

Digilari turned the brief into a seasonal series and tightened every part of the targeting:

A seasonal campaign series

New Year's ran in three creative phases from February to May 2026. Summer Campaign 2026 followed in August with the same “prepare before the season” angle: protect vehicles, machinery, boats and caravans from summer sun and storms.

Local targeting around 22 towns

Ads reached people within 25 miles of 22 Central Victorian towns, including Bendigo, Ballarat, Castlemaine and Echuca, with Melbourne excluded. Interests matched shed buyers: farming and machinery, boating, caravans and 4WD, DIY and home improvement, plus the Recently moved life event.

Three formats tested

Single image, carousel and video ads launched side by side. The single image won clearly (3.12% click-through rate at $0.18 per click), and Meta put 81% of September's budget behind it.

Cleaner placements and tracking

Audience Network placements were excluded, Advantage+ creative enhancements were switched on, and UTM parameters were standardised so GA4 attributes Meta visits correctly.

Results

Cheaper, More Relevant Visits Ahead of Summer

  • Click-through rate: 2.75%, against 1.85% on the previous seasonal campaign.
  • Cost per click: $0.22, down from $0.37.
  • Cost per landing page view: $0.37, down from $0.44, across 2,190 landing page views.
  • Best month: September 2026, at a 3.63% click-through rate, $0.16 per click and $0.30 per landing page view.

This is an awareness and website traffic campaign. Enquiries are captured mainly through Google Ads, so this case study reports visits rather than leads.
