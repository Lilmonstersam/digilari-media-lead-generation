About Cosmetics Plus

Cosmetics Plus is a beauty retailer offering cosmetics, self-care products and in-store piercing services through its local stores. Social media supports both its online reputation and foot traffic to each store.

Challenges

When Cosmetics Plus came on board it had very little social media presence. Its Facebook page was largely inactive, and there were several duplicate pages and negative reviews that needed attention.

Before the brand could grow online, its digital footprint had to be cleaned up, its pages consolidated and its customer feedback addressed.

Solutions

Digilari fixed the foundations first, then built a content programme on top of them:

Page clean-up and consolidation

Claimed the duplicate and existing public business pages and consolidated them into one official Facebook page.

Reputation management

Responded to the backlog of reviews and complaints, de-escalated where possible, and wrote a standard process for answering every type of review, from positive feedback to complaints.

Consistent community replies

Created scripts for frequently asked questions and repeat enquiries, so replies stayed consistent across platforms before chatbots were widely available.

Content beyond promotions

Aligned social content with the promotional calendar, then filled the gaps with self-care tips, piercing education and product features. Reels moved from slideshows to creative videos with transitions, movement and music.

Focused channels and local promotion

Narrowed active posting from Facebook, Instagram, X and Pinterest to Facebook and Instagram, while keeping Pinterest boards up to date. Paid piercing promotions reached people within 20 km of each store.

Results

One Official Page, a Clear Process and More Engaging Content

  • Consolidated presence: duplicate pages resolved into one official Facebook page.
  • Reputation: the backlog of reviews and complaints answered, with a documented process for handling feedback from then on.
  • Community management: consistent, scripted replies to common questions across platforms.
  • Content: reworked Reels performed well with the algorithm and increased reach and impressions.
