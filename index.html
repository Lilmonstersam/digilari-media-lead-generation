    What Does a Lead Generation Agency Do?

    A lead generation agency finds, attracts and qualifies potential customers for your sales team. Some agencies sell shared leads or run cold-calling. Digilari Media builds an inbound pipeline that you own: we put your business in front of buyers while they research, capture their enquiry, and pass it to your team with the source, intent and value attached.

    We are based in Brisbane and Melbourne and work with B2B businesses right across Australia.

    A strong fit if you

    • Sell high-value products or services to other businesses
    • Have a sales cycle of weeks or months, with several decision-makers
    • Want enquiries from estimators, specifiers, procurement and project managers
    • Want to own your leads, ad accounts and data

    Not the right fit if you

    • Want to buy residential job leads per enquiry
    • Need purchased contact lists or cold-calling

      Lead Generation FAQs

      What is a lead generation agency?

      A lead generation agency attracts and qualifies potential customers for your sales team. At Digilari Media, that means building a B2B inbound pipeline with Google Ads, LinkedIn, SEO and landing pages, then nurturing and reporting on every lead through to sales-qualified stage. We do not buy, sell or share lead lists.

      How much does lead generation cost in Australia?

      It depends on the model. Pay-per-lead providers charge for each contact, which is often shared with competitors. Agencies like ours charge a monthly retainer plus ad spend, and you own every lead. Our retainers start from $2,500 per month, and most B2B lead generation clients choose the Multi-Channel Lead Engine from $5,500 per month.

      How long does it take to get qualified leads?

      Google Ads and LinkedIn campaigns usually start producing enquiries within weeks of launch. SEO and content build more slowly over several months, then lower your cost per lead because they keep working without extra ad spend.

      Do you sell or share leads?

      No. Every enquiry comes through your own website, ad accounts and CRM. The leads, data and accounts are yours, including if you ever stop working with us.

      What types of businesses do you generate leads for?

      B2B businesses with considered, high-value sales: commercial construction suppliers, specialist contractors, manufacturers, and mining, logistics and agricultural suppliers. We are not the right fit for buying residential job leads.

      How is B2B lead generation different from B2C?

      B2B purchases involve longer sales cycles, larger deal sizes and several decision-makers. That calls for precise targeting by role and industry, content that supports specification and approval, and nurture sequences that keep you front of mind until the buyer is ready.

      What is the difference between lead generation and account-based marketing?

      Lead generation attracts and qualifies any buyer who is actively searching for what you sell. Account-based marketing (ABM) targets a defined list of high-value companies and engages their whole buying committee with personalised LinkedIn ads, content and outreach. Lead generation fills the pipeline; ABM wins the specific accounts that matter most. Many B2B businesses run both together. Learn about our account-based marketing services.

      How do you measure lead quality?

      We track every form and call to its source, integrate with your CRM and report on marketing-qualified leads, sales-qualified leads and pipeline value, so you can see which channels produce revenue and not just clicks.

      What is the Digital Results Guarantee?

      It is our commitment to agreed KPIs. If we do not hit the lead targets we set together within the agreed timeframe, our management fees pause until we do. It is available on our value-based retainers.
