FAQs

Questions before we start?

Every programme starts with your market, sales process and account list. These are the questions we hear most often.

What is account-based marketing?

ABM is a B2B strategy that focuses marketing and sales activity on a selected group of valuable accounts. Messaging and channels are planned around each account's buying group and commercial opportunity.

How is ABM different from lead generation?

Lead generation usually starts with an individual enquiry. ABM starts with the companies you want to win, then builds engagement across the people involved in the purchase. The two approaches can run together.

Do we need a target account list?

No. We can build one with your sales team using your best customers, target sectors, deal economics and available market data. A clear list is agreed before campaigns launch.

How do you measure an ABM programme?

We look at account reach and engagement, buying-group coverage, meetings, opportunities and pipeline progression. The mix depends on your sales cycle and CRM data.
