The snapshots include service pages, agency homepages and directories for the broad social media query. The three platform queries are predominantly dedicated service pages. The page structure therefore uses one broad service hub and three focused service pages, each with a distinct title, H1, service detail, budget explanation, FAQs and contact path.

## Pages reviewed and content decisions

- [Impressive: Social Media Marketing](https://impressive.com.au/social-media-marketing/) ranks for the broad agency query. Its page covers strategy, paid social, content, community management, reporting, platform-specific services and client proof. The Digilari hub covers these service questions while keeping navigation shorter and linking to the three platform pages.
- [Rocket: LinkedIn Ads](https://rocketagency.com.au/services/social-media-ads-agency/linkedin-ads) ranks for LinkedIn advertising agency. It addresses decision-maker targeting, ad formats, small-budget suitability, costs and how LinkedIn supports longer B2B sales cycles. The Digilari LinkedIn page answers these intents and links to the verified Topdrill campaign.
- [Dilate: Instagram Ads Management](https://www.dilate.com.au/instagram-ads-management/) ranks for Instagram Ads agency. It focuses on creative formats, audience targeting, ecommerce integration and reporting. The Digilari page treats Reels, Stories, Feed, conversion journeys and creative testing as the core service questions.
- [SOUP: Facebook Ads](https://soupagency.com.au/social-media/facebook-ads/) ranks for Facebook advertising agency. It covers lead ads, ecommerce, creative, measurement, costs and the relationship between Facebook and Instagram placements. The Digilari page includes those decision points without borrowing competitor benchmarks or making unsupported performance claims.

## Case-study evidence

The published [Topdrill case study](https://digilari.com.au/case-studies/topdrill/) documents a LinkedIn Ads recruitment campaign with 2,461 clicks from 85,024 impressions and a 2.89% click-through rate. It is the only published Digilari case study found that clearly identifies a social advertising channel and its measured result. Instagram and Facebook case-study slots are explicitly labelled placeholders. They contain no fabricated client names or results.
